CAiSE'27 invites proposals for tutorials offering new insights, knowledge, and skills to professionals, educators, researchers, and students on advanced topics in the field of information systems engineering.

Proposals addressing this year's conference theme, "**Hybrid Intelligent Information Systems Engineering**", are encouraged. Proposals on other emerging or established topics in information systems engineering are equally welcome.

Tutorials should enhance participants' understanding of principles, concepts, methods, and tools relevant to the CAiSE community. Tutorials must be educational rather than commercial or sales-oriented.

Tutorials will be 90 minutes long and will run in parallel with the technical sessions of the main conference. Proposals should identify the intended audience and any prerequisite knowledge.

## Selection Criteria

The tutorial chairs will review proposals based on the following criteria:

- Relevance to the field of IS engineering;
- Anticipated appeal to the conference audience;
- Timeliness and importance for the conference audience;
- Past experience and qualifications of the instructor(s).

The tutorial chairs will also consider the complementarity of each proposal with respect to the conference program and other tutorial proposals.

## Submission guidelines

Tutorial proposals should be submitted to EasyChair using the conference submission site: [CAiSE 2027 Tutorials](https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=caise2027) and then selecting the "CAiSE2027 Tutorials Track".

Proposals should be submitted in PDF format and must not exceed 1,500 words, excluding references and presenter biographies. Each proposal should cover the following points:

- Title
- Presenters: Names, affiliations, and short biographies highlighting relevant expertise for the tutorial topic.
- Goal: The overall goal of the tutorial and what participants are expected to learn.
- Scope: Intended audience, level (basic or advanced), and prerequisites.
- Topic relevance: Relevance to CAiSE and the intended audience, including the conference theme where applicable.
- Content outline: A structured overview of the planned tutorial contents.
- Previous delivery: If the tutorial has been presented before, indicate the venues and any planned updates.
- References: Relevant papers, books, and other supporting materials.
- Sample slides (optional): Presenters may include sample slides or a link to existing tutorial materials.

## Publication and Presenter Support

- A 2-page tutorial abstract will be published in the CAiSE LNCS proceedings.
- Tutorials will benefit from the local organizational infrastructure, including registration, badges, refreshments, and equipment.
- Promotion of the tutorial on the CAiSE 2027 website and through conference mailings.
- A conference registration discount per accepted tutorial.

## Questions and Inquiries

Questions about eligibility and other inquiries can be sent to the CAiSE'27 Tutorial Chairs at [caise2027-tutorials@easychair.org](mailto:caise2027-tutorials@easychair.org).
