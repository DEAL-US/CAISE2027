The deadline to apply for the CAiSE 2027 PhD Award is **19 March 2027**. The conditions to apply are:

- having participated as an author in a previous CAiSE Doctoral Consortium or at a main CAiSE event: either the main conference, the CAiSE Forum, EMMSAD, or BPMDS **and**
- having successfully defended the PhD thesis in the last two years (i.e., since January 2025).

The application must be a single PDF file containing:

- A short cover letter that includes the list of PhD committee members, and a list of publications that resulted from the PhD thesis, highlighting those that make the application eligible.
- The support letter from the thesis advisor.
- The candidate's defended PhD thesis.
- The candidate's CV.
- Any additional information the candidate deems relevant.

The application must be submitted electronically to the **PhD Award track** of CAiSE 2027 via [EasyChair](https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=caise2027).

## About the PhD Award

The CAiSE PhD Award is granted annually to an outstanding recent PhD thesis in the field of Information Systems Engineering. The award is co-sponsored by the CAiSE Steering Committee and Springer. It consists of a certificate, free full registration (5 days) to the next two editions of the CAiSE conference, and a book voucher for a free selection worth EUR 500 from Springer's printed books collection.

In addition, the selected thesis will be recommended for publication as a monograph in the LNBIP series published by Springer, provided that Springer's publication conditions are met.

The PhD theses submitted for the award will be reviewed by a standing committee of senior members selected from the CAiSE Advisory Committee, the CAiSE Steering Committee, and the CAiSE Program Committee.

The review process is guided by the following selection criteria:

- Scientific contribution.
- Relevance of the contributions for information systems engineering research and practice.
- The way this relevance has been addressed by the PhD Award candidate in the papers and PhD text.
- List of publications that resulted from the PhD research, with particular attention for papers published at CAiSE, including Doctoral Consortium papers.
- Recommendation by the supervisor. This recommendation can include the opinion of another external member of the SC if deemed relevant by the supervisor.
- Any other relevant evidence provided by the candidate.
