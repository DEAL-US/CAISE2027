## Registration Fees

The following fees apply to the three registration categories. Student rates are subject to the conference's student-registration requirements.

### Early Registration (until May 2, 2027)

| Registration category | Fee |
| --- | ---: |
| Full Registration – Regular | 950 € |
| Full Registration – Student | 700 € |
| Conference-only Registration – Regular | 750 € |
| Conference-only Registration – Student | 600 € |
| Co-located Events Registration – Regular | 430 € |
| Co-located Events Registration – Student | 350 € |

### Late Registration (after May 2, 2027)

Late registration fees include a surcharge of 150 € on top of the corresponding early registration fee.

## Additional Papers

A surcharge of 150 € applies for each additional paper having the same presenting and registering author.

## Extra Tickets for Companions

Companions of conference participants may purchase extra tickets to attend the social events. The prices below are per ticket. Extra tickets are intended for companions and are subject to availability.

* **Monday, 7 – Co-located Events Reception:** 80 €
* **Tuesday, 8 – Conference Reception:** 80 €
* **Wednesday, 9 – Gala Dinner:** 100 €
* **Thursday, 10 – Conference Party:** 80 €
